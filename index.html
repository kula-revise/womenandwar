Women and war. Build the essay.

Start with what you know and finish with a full essay in your own words. You revise the content, plan the essay, write it one paragraph at a time, and get feedback on each part before you move on.

The question you will answer

Paper 2, Topic 11: Causes and effects of 20th-century wars. An essay marked out of 15. You have about 45 minutes for it in the exam.

How it works
1

Understand and revise

Work out what the question is asking. Then revise women's role and status in the First World War and the Spanish Civil War, and what historians argue about them.

2

Decide and plan

Match four themes to the evidence for them. Weigh them, decide your thesis and plan each paragraph.

3

Write and check

Write your introduction, each paragraph and your conclusion from your plan. Check each one and fix it.

4

Send your essay

Copy your finished essay or save it as a file, then send it to your teacher.

Your coachYou are not working alone. At any step you can ask the coach a question, find out more about a fact, and have your writing checked before you move on.
